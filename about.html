About Stackly Beauty

Redefining beauty standards through premium, ethically-sourced cosmetics and skincare solutions.

Our Story

Founded in 2018, Stackly Beauty emerged from a passion to make premium beauty products accessible to everyone. What started as a small boutique in Salem has evolved into a trusted destination for beauty enthusiasts across the region.

Our founder, inspired by the diverse beauty needs of different skin types, curated a collection of cruelty-free, sustainable products that celebrate natural beauty and promote self-confidence.

Today, we serve thousands of satisfied customers who trust us with their daily beauty rituals and special occasions.

Our Mission

To empower individuals of all skin types with innovative, high-quality beauty products that enhance natural beauty while promoting sustainable and ethical practices.

Our Vision

To become the leading beauty brand that bridges the gap between luxury and accessibility, inspiring confidence and self-expression in every customer.

Our Values

Quality, integrity, sustainability, and inclusivity form the foundation of everything we do. We believe beauty is for everyone, and everyone deserves the best.

Our Ethical Sourcing Commitment

We believe true beauty shouldn't come at the expense of our planet. That’s why every ingredient in the Stackly collection is responsibly sourced from sustainable farms and ethical cooperatives around the world.

By prioritizing fair trade and organic farming methods, we ensure that our products are not only gentle on your skin, but also gentle on the communities that help create them.

  • Support for Local Farmers
  • Zero Carbon Footprint Manufacturing
