Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?

We offer 30 days return on all products if they are unused and in original packaging. To initiate a return, please contact our support team with your order number.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping is available for 24-48 hour delivery. Free shipping on orders above ₹999.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes, all our products are 100% cruelty-free and certified by recognized organizations. We never test on animals and partner only with ethical suppliers.

Can I get a personalized beauty consultation?

Absolutely! We offer free virtual consultations with our beauty experts. Book a slot through the Services page or call our store directly to schedule.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we ship within India. International shipping is coming soon. Subscribe to our newsletter to be notified when this service becomes available.

How can I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. You can use this to track your package on our website or the courier's portal.
